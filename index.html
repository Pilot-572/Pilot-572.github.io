01 / About

Creative Brand Chartreuse

Building things.
Breaking limits.

My name is Fabian, and I have a passion for engineering — a different kind. What can a 14-year-old possibly build? Quite a lot, as it turns out. From soldering electronics to designing parts in CAD, I've spent my time making things rather than just reading about them.

From a young age I had an addiction to cars and speed. I have a year of professional karting under my belt, and many more years of sim racing on top of that. That obsession pushed me to build my own simulator wheelbase and steering wheel from scratch, because if I want it done right, I do it myself. Speed, electronics, engineering. Now I'm looking at making the journey from my small town near Botoșani, Romania, all the way to the UK, and hopefully, to Silverstone UTC, where I want to take all of this next.

04 / Brand

Chartreuse.

My personal creative brand — started as a joke that stayed. It began with CRT (Chartreuse Racing Team), then grew into two more branches. The name came from a classmate who found the colour online during a Robotics camp at Logiscool. That was the day Chartreuse became mine.

My close friend also uses CCT for school projects, which makes it feel more real than it probably should.

CRT
Chartreuse Racing Team

The sim racing side of Chartreuse. Competed in iRacing official races with a custom-made livery on the Porsche Cayman 718. Team theme: Missing — Todd Terry Club Mix.

CCT
Chartreuse Creativity Team

The visual and cinematic side. BeamNG.drive car edits, video work, whatever else I feel like making. If it's Chartreuse and it's not a race or a troop, it's probably CCT.

CTIT
Chartreuse Tactical Intervention Team

My Ready Or Not troop. My friends have ICON troops and META troops, and I genuinely don't know what those stand for. It's purely fictional — just an extra branch for Chartreuse.
